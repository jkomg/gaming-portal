# Sana “Mercy” Pell

Plain medical coat, rolled sleeves, a case with more patches than labels. Names, pronouns, and appearance are flexible. This character is already part of the crew.

## Core action ratings

| Attribute | Actions |

| Insight | Doctor 2; Hack 1; Rig 0; Study 1 |
| Prowess | Helm 0; Scramble 1; Scrap 0; Skulk 0 |

**Resistance:** Insight 3d; Prowess 1d; Resolve 2d before ability-specific bonuses. **Vice:** 1d. Count actions with a dot for attributes; do not add resistance-only bonuses to vice.

**Unique starting ability — I’m a Doctor, Not a…:** Push to use your Doctor rating for a different action, explaining the patient, research, or posting that taught the trick. The normal push benefit applies as in the core rules.

**Chosen special ability — Physicker:** Study ailments, injuries, bodies, and crime scenes. Crew recovery rolls gain +1d when you help with treatment. This stacks with the ship’s Medical Bay bonus; it does not grant the separate Patch ability.

You have both abilities. No Xeno substitution or Veteran ability was selected. These are paraphrases of the S&V abilities; use your supplied core playbook for the complete text.

## Your connections

- **Heritage:** Imperial; family served a district hospital on Warren.
- **Background:** Academic; completed medical training, then left a restrictive research grant.
- **Close friend:** Alben, a former patient. You kept their care confidential.
- **Rival:** Lynie, a hospital administrator. You refused a politically useful diagnosis.
- **Vice:** Obligation; run a small free clinic rota through Juda, a doctor (campaign relationship).
- **Crew tie:** Fuse repairs the equipment you cannot afford to replace; you make sure they occasionally eat.
- **Drive:** Keep treatment from becoming leverage over patients.

Published playbook contacts are used with original campaign relationships. Neither friends nor unchosen pregens are free cohorts.
