## Your connections

- **Heritage:** Wanderer; raised between stations and shrines.
- **Background:** Cult; left a doctrinal school over how it treated Urbots.
- **Close friend:** Horux, a former teacher. They still answer your questions.
- **Rival:** Blish, a fellow mystic. They think your compassion compromises discipline.
- **Vice:** Weird; listening to low-level Way echoes with Hicks, a mystic supplier (campaign use of a published contact).
- **Crew tie:** Anchor trusts you with fears they will not name. You helped Mercy keep a frightened patient calm.
- **Drive:** Learn what the sector is trying to tell people nobody listens to.

Published playbook contacts are used with original campaign relationships. Neither friends nor unchosen pregens are free cohorts.
