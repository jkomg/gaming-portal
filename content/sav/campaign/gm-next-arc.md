# GM: After the quiet orbit

**Optional follow-on opportunities for the core S&V learning run.** Nothing here is required to explain the teaching job. Start from what the crew did and whom they now care about. These notes are private; let public discoveries enter the wiki through play.

## The larger question

Who can claim ownership of a route, an Urbot, or a living intelligence? Morrow's remembered signal may connect isolated habitats that cannot afford Guild-controlled transport. It is a lead, not a prophecy or a key that alone destroys the Hegemony. If Morrow is an animal, the same signal appears in its old tracking harness and research records; do not force intelligence back into the chosen version.

Transfer the social pressure behind Blades' new factions into **existing S&V powers**. Conclave 01 already gives created people an autonomy movement. Cobalt Syndicate supplies labor and transport pressure. The Guilds have commercial and technical interests. The Church can offer a sincere, dangerous interpretation of a Way phenomenon. Add individual advocates and disagreements rather than giving each faction one moral personality.

## Three people with incompatible offers

- **Tess-9, Conclave 01 advocate:** wants the archive copied without publishing identities of fugitives using its routes. Offers technical assistance and shelter, not free advanced ship systems. Wants Morrow treated as a participant if intelligent.
- **Overseer Ilex, Guild negotiator:** wants exclusive route surveys to prevent untested transit disasters; also expects profitable monopoly. Offers a legal charter and removal of a specific equipment claim. Can honor a negotiated deal; is not secretly behind every event.
- **Sister Vael, Church navigator:** believes the signal is evidence of a sacred boundary and wants a supervised expedition. Offers charts and a volunteer, with explicit restrictions on artifact handling. Some of the warnings may be justified.

The crew's Cobalt contact **Jori Pell** calls in the already established favor: carry a habitat workers' representative and replacement seals. That job must remain viable even if the crew declines every mystery above. Counters Guild −2 creates paperwork and creditor pressure, not instant war or an unavoidable seizure.

## Opportunity 1: Salvage with voices

A derelict relay in **Holt** has a fragment matching the signal. Client can be Tess-9 or a Guild surveyor, depending on the crew's choice. Job: salvage the navigation spool and recover any survivors. Advertise **8 CRED** plus clearly stated salvage rights. The extra travel allows an actual different-system lay-low activity later, but don't pretend local Rin travel already did so.

Obstacles: unstable rotation; a rescue beacon replaying old distress calls; a salvage claimant with a valid partial title. Discovery: an independent Urbot has kept the relay alive and wants safe passage, not theft of their memory. No need to steal a person's core to get the navigation fragment; copying, bargaining, repairs, or retrieving a backup are possible. If the crew ignores the signal, use this as an ordinary salvage contract instead.

## Opportunity 2: A witness, not cargo

The workers' representative on Jori's favor run has evidence of unsafe Guild routing. The job is safe transport and a chosen hearing destination, not compulsory revolutionary recruitment. The Cobalt obligation is paid by this delivery, not an infinite chain of favors. Offer **4 CRED expenses/pay** so “favor” is not a secret upkeep trap. Say who pays, how much, and when before engagement.

Obstacles: a competing courier offers a fast route with a catch; the witness wants to stop at an endangered habitat; a customs office requests a lawful interview. The crew decides whose promise matters. Evidence can support a public hearing, private settlement, or independent distribution. Those outcomes change faction status through named actions, not a prewritten betrayal.

## Opportunity 3: The route belongs to whom?

A buyer offers **10 CRED** to demonstrate a reconstructed local bypass at a public shipping auction. Tess-9 asks for an anonymous safe-access copy. Vael warns that one section crosses an active Way disturbance. Ilex offers paid licensing instead. Telegraph that testing the dangerous route can put lives at risk; there is a slower safe route with weaker commercial value. No unannounced mandatory gate-damage consequence.

Obstacles: secure the relay calibration; negotiate who accompanies the demonstration; choose what to publish and what to conceal. A successful job can end with an independent cooperative route, a licensed route, a hidden rescue corridor, or the crew rejecting the whole deal. The archive is a useful contested asset, not an excuse to award free crew quality or ignore module slots.

## Run clocks between jobs

Use four six-segment faction clocks, initially 0, adjusted from the actual starter outcome:

- **Guild exclusive charter:** advances when offices secure surveys and signatures. Full means a legal claim and enforcement offer; the crew can contest it.
- **Conclave safe corridor:** advances when advocates gain willing partners and secure copies. Full creates a usable safe network, not automatic sector liberation.
- **Church quarantine case:** advances when credible danger reports accumulate or Vael secures backing. Full means an attempted restriction; negotiate, defy, or investigate.
- **Habitat supply failure:** advances only after named disruptions or unattended needs, not because the GM wants urgency. Full creates a concrete shortage and rescue job, not offscreen mass death by default.

After each job, choose relevant faction actions and use the core fortune procedure where uncertain. Announce what the crew could reasonably learn. Not every clock ticks every downtime. Advance one or two pressures that actually moved. Hostility should produce offers, obstacles, and choices before an unavoidable confrontation.

## Keep learning through play

Continue with core S&V. Introduce rules as they become relevant, and use the session log to record what the crew learns and changes.
