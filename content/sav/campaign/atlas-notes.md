# Local route atlas

[Open the atlas](/sav/atlas/). It is an interactive schematic for **A Breathing Cargo**, not a canonical map of the Rin system. Kestrel Transfer and Lantern Clinic are original locations near Warren. The inspection corridor is a local traffic-control route, not an inter-system gate. All job nodes are in Rin; traveling between them does not qualify as laying low in another system.

Choose a site in the diagram or the keyboard-accessible directory. Filter by the crew, station operations, customs, independent medical care, or local context. These labels describe access and jurisdiction at the start of the job, not exclusive faction territory. The map contains only facts the crew can know from its briefing; private cargo behavior and later plot leads stay in staff-only pages.

- **Warren:** published Rin location; context for this small freight route, not a claim to map every port.
- **Kestrel bonded desk:** pickup authorization and release records; Mira is the useful first contact.
- **Quiet Orbit berth:** the crew's ship, load access, and departure point.
- **Inspection corridor:** manifest inspection and outbound clearance; a medical escort can be negotiated.
- **Lantern Clinic:** independent receiving medical ship beyond the inspection perimeter, still inside Rin.

Lines show a known route, not the only legal player choice. Negotiate a tow, take another service lane, or return safely to Sen if the situation calls for it. Map placement does not establish a hidden obstacle. The [people page](/sav/wiki/people-and-factions) and [ship record](/sav/wiki/quiet-orbit) list actual starting faction statuses.

The diagram can grow after play: add locations and public faction influence when the crew learns them. Do not embed unrevealed GM clocks or secrets in a supposedly player-safe static JSON file.
