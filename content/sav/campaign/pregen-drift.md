**Chosen special ability — Traveler:** Potency when you Consort with or Sway unusual cultures or xenos. Describe what lets you bridge the difference; it does not give a universal extra die.

You have both abilities. No Xeno substitution or Veteran ability was selected. These are paraphrases of the S&V abilities; use your supplied core playbook for the complete text.

## Your connections

- **Heritage:** Spacer; raised on ice-haulers.
- **Background:** Labor; former tug pilot, dismissed for towing a stranded ship without permission.
- **Close friend:** Triv, a ship mechanic. You always return borrowed equipment.
- **Rival:** Choss, a professional racer. You refused to throw a race.
- **Vice:** Gambling; low-stakes flight-simulator wagers at Alor’s stopover bar (campaign placement).
- **Crew tie:** Fuse saved your engine during the air-scrubber run; you got Anchor out before the dock shutters closed.
- **Drive:** Keep the Quiet Orbit flying without becoming someone’s owned transport.

Published playbook contacts are used with original campaign relationships. Neither friends nor unchosen pregens are free cohorts.
