# GM: A Breathing Cargo

**Core S&V only • 3–5 PCs • 2–3 hours • private preparation.** Read the player brief and ship record first. This job teaches a complete loop, including payoff and downtime. It is a situation with several exits, not a required sequence of rolls.

## The job-table recipe

These are deliberately selected results, not a claim that dice were rolled: the Stardancer's lucrative opportunity **living cargo, difficult to contain** (core p. 127); civilian client **academic/scholar** and underworld work **smuggle/courier** (p. 338); complications **watched by Hegemonic forces** and **strong Precursor/Way presence** (p. 339). Kestrel Transfer, Lantern Clinic, and all job-specific SPCs are original additions to Rin. Citani and Warren are published setting elements.

## What is really in the case

**Morrow** is a living, bioelectric organism recovered from an abandoned research habitat. It senses patterns in the Way and repeats familiar signals through nearby maintenance circuits. It is intelligent enough to express preferences, frightened, and trying to find quiet. Its habitat has a paper trail that multiple offices claim authority over. Sen knows it is alive and unusual; Sen has not recognized its intelligence. There is no secret plan to sell it after the crew does the right thing.

An automated customs interrogation broadcast agitates Morrow. It starts reproducing that pattern in nearby nonessential systems; connected equipment may open an access panel or wake an escape pod. The case is not a magical remote hack of any ship at any distance. Physical isolation, a filtered power supply, medical stabilization, changing the broadcast pattern, or communication can all help. No Mystic is required. If the table chose an animal version, use learned responses instead of words, with the same practical solutions.

If Silk uses Player, give an honest assessment: Sen intends medical care and payment; Sen is withholding no known sale or ambush. The paperwork dispute is real. Do not invalidate a lie-reading ability to preserve a twist.

## Pacing

| Minutes | Aim |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Choose pregens, crew tie, boundaries |
| 10–20 | Brief, load, one useful information question, plan and engagement |
| 20–45 | Collect the case; teach one action and consequence |
| 45–80 | Inspection or a route around it; reveal cargo behavior |
| 80–100 | Delivery, withdrawal, or another chosen resolution |
| 100–135 | Payoff, upkeep, Heat, entanglement, two activities, XP |
| 135–150 | Debrief and choose a next lead |

For a strict two hours, end the job by minute 80 and reserve at least 30 minutes for aftermath. Cut an obstacle, never the whole downtime lesson. Five players should get one meaningful decision each, not five compulsory rolls at every station.

## Opening at the obstacle

Read: *The bonded desk light turns amber as your pickup code scans. Beyond the glass, a trunk-sized habitat case breathes cool vapor. A public address asks all outbound traffic to keep comms open for inspection. The clerk looks from the code to your ship registration: “Medical transfer? I can release it. Your exit clearance is another department.”*

Confirm the agreed **6 CRED** live delivery, **3 CRED** safe return to Sen, and known **1 CRED upkeep**. A transport plan needs a route and means. Use the normal engagement pool: start at 1d, apply the core fictional factors, and add The Getaway's +1d if this is a delivery plan. A straightforward neutral transport can therefore start at 2d; adjust from the actual plan, not an invented mandatory penalty. On a 6 begin controlled, 4/5 risky, 1–3 desperate; critical begins beyond the opening obstacle. Do not roll engagement again for every scene.

At controlled entry, Mira has cleared the pickup and asks a routine question. Risky: clearance is paused while she asks why the destination changed to a ship. Desperate: a customs inspection team is already at the pallet; begin with their imminent seizure stated openly. These are starting situations, not extra harm or Heat automatically inflicted by engagement.

## Three pressures, several answers

**The handoff.** Clerk Mira needs a defensible release record. Sway can frame the legitimate medical urgency; Consort can use a relevant relationship; Hack can redirect the clearance queue; Skulk can use maintenance access. If the code and explanation plainly suffice, release the cargo without rolling. A risky partial success might get release but alert inspector Hale (Inspection lock +1), or require a documented guarantor. Failure may leave the case behind the desk with a new demand; never delete every possible approach.

**The corridor.** Hale wants a complete manifest, not a shootout. Official transfer, a changed registry through the Fake Transponder, a tow arrangement with Asha, disabling the local broadcast, a quiet maintenance departure, and a blockade run are all possible. Typical station security is quality I; it is not automatically the full Tier of every Guild in the sector. Describe any actual superior equipment before it constrains effect. Scramble/Helm escapes receive The Getaway potency. Drift's Ace Pilot and the crew ability may reinforce the same fictional advantage; do not mechanically multiply identical potency into automatic extreme effect.

**The cargo.** As the interrogation broadcast begins, the habitat taps a slow pattern against its frame. A display draws a route toward the clinic. Offer that information before asking how PCs investigate. Study/Doctor can establish a meaningful response; Rig can isolate supply; Hack can redirect the interrogation system; Attune can reach a mind through the Way. Normal human kindness plus clear gestures can work without a roll when nothing contests it. The complication becomes hazardous only if pressure is active and the response uncertain. The party can simply take the powered case to safe care.

## Only two clocks

Show **Inspection lock 0/4** and **Habitat distress 0/4**. They measure fictional developments, not elapsed table minutes. Announce the clock and number of ticks as a consequence before resistance; use 1/2/3 ticks for minor/moderate/severe consequences when appropriate. No automatic tick for every roll, every six, or every real-time interval.

- Inspection lock full: the regular departure lane closes and Hale orders the ship held. Alternatives remain: negotiate medical release, accept an escort, use the maintenance lane with a new danger, or run the blockade. It does not retroactively confiscate the case or disable the ship.
- Habitat distress full: Morrow opens its outer service housing and reaches for the nearest powered refuge. This exposes the creature and changes the transport problem; it does not kill it. Offer portable life support, containment repair, moving together into the ship's medical bay, or the escape pod as a revised route.

Use an immediate consequence instead of a clock tick where that is clearer: level 1 “arc burn,” a dropped tool, lost authorization, or worsening position. Avoid charging harm, Heat, a clock, and lost effect for one mild consequence. Choose a consequence, then let resistance or armor do its work.

## Teach through one example

Fuse bypasses a service latch with Hack 2. GM: risky/standard; a 4/5 opens it and advances Inspection lock by 2. Fuse pushes (+1d, 2 stress), Silk assists (+1d, 1 stress), and the crew spends a Gambit (+1d): 5d, highest kept. On a 5, the latch opens and the warning activates. Fuse can resist the warning with Insight 3d (Hack, Rig, Study have dots). A highest 5 costs 1 stress; the GM says whether the warning is reduced to one tick or prevented by the established fiction. Because a Gambit was spent, even a 6 would not replenish one from this roll.

Offer a flashback when a player says “I wish we'd prepared a medical export declaration.” Quote a stress cost before the flashback. A perfectly plausible existing blank form might be 0; arranging a compliant official under pressure might be 1–2 and require a roll. It cannot undo the fact Hale has already read the manifest. Prompt a setup or assist if someone wants to help; do not force each PC into the same action.

## Endings and payment

The clinic will accept a living intelligent occupant as a patient, not inventory. Dr. Olan asks for the case history and the crew's consent to describe what happened. Respect the crew's boundaries about research and custody. Sen pays 6 for a living delivery in the case; if the crew makes a medically necessary alternate safe transfer, Sen honors the same fee. If they choose the advertised safe return, pay 3. Walking away without delivery or safe return yields no job payment, but do not remove other earned resources. A dead occupant means the agreed success condition was not met; discuss how the fiction reached that outcome, never invent a lethal “clock full” surprise.

Negotiating freedom, a future visit, or a different caretaker is possible. Do not require a moral speech or the “correct” ending. Escorted delivery, paperwork-heavy delivery, a chaotic escape, and quiet success are all valid sessions.

## Run the aftermath on screen

1. **Payoff:** normally 6 CRED. No Blades Rep award. Existing ship funds 2 + fee 6 − upkeep 1 = 7; distribute/spend at least 3 to respect ship capacity 4. Each PC also has personal capacity 4. Record actual shares.
2. **Upkeep:** pay 1 from ship funds; if skipped, use core skipped-upkeep/debt rules rather than inventing an extra tithe. Fuse's repair ability does not waive upkeep.
3. **Heat in Rin:** choose the core band from what happened: 0 contained/quiet, 1 smooth, 2 normal, 4 loud, 6 devastating; apply relevant core modifiers, including high profile/artifacts +1, Hegemonic citizen killing or mass destruction/illegal weapons +2, gate/hyperlane damage +3. A quiet permit fix is not automatically a loud job. At 8 advance Wanted, clear eight, retain overflow. Other systems stay unchanged.
4. **Factions:** explain any change earned by a consequential choice. Clinic access is not automatically Mendicant membership. Honoring the Cobalt favor can happen later; don't charge it again at payoff.
5. **Entanglement:** in Rin, roll one d6 in its current Wanted column (normally 0); 6 escalates to the next table as the book directs. Use the actual result, reskinned to known people. Allow the crew to address it through play. Do not replace the roll with a mandatory chase.
6. **Downtime:** each active PC gets two activities because the crew is not at war; extras cost 1 CRED. Use real stress/harm from play; demonstrate recovery only if someone is hurt. Medical Bay +1d and Mercy's applicable Physicker bonus can stack. Fuse has the extra repair activity and free repair supplied by Bailing Wire. Silk's extra activity is acquire asset/lay low only; lay low requires another system. A safe local return to the clinic does not meet that requirement.
7. **XP:** read character and crew triggers aloud. Training normally grants XP; core recovery is a six-clock clearing all harm.
