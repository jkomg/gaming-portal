**Completed Stardancer crew | Professional reputation | CF-350 Scarab-class freighter**

You are partners already. Last month you delivered replacement air scrubbers to a habitat that could not afford a Guild contract. Citani has offered the crew another introduction. Nobody has to recruit the others during this session.

**Upkeep is 1 CRED:** floor((Engines 2 + Hull 2 + Comms 1 + Weapons 0 + crew quality 0) / 4) = 1. Use the arithmetic for this actual ship, not a generic suggested starting upkeep. A full 6-CRED payoff plus the existing 2 leaves 7 after upkeep; distribute or spend at least 3 before the next job because the ship normally holds only 4 CRED.

## What the ship can do

**The Getaway:** the whole crew has potency when using Scramble or Helm to escape capture or run a blockade. Delivery jobs get +1d on engagement. It does not give a die on every Helm roll. Core p. 124.

**Fake Transponder:** offers a false ship identity; it does not magically fix every inconsistent cargo manifest. **Smuggling Compartments:** provide concealed cargo storage; a sufficiently focused search can still find it. **Medical Bay:** +1d to crew recovery rolls. **Insight Training:** a training activity on that attribute marks 2 XP instead of 1. No workshop, AI module, armed escort, or free cohort is included.

## Creation accounting

The base Stardancer has Engines 1, Hull 2, Comms 0, Weapons 0 and crew quality 0, plus Jump Drive, Cargo Hold, Smuggling Compartments, Galley, and Insight Training. We selected Engines +1 and Comms +1 for the two system improvements. The two additional upgrades are Fake Transponder and Medical Bay; the Comms rating provides the transponder's module slot.

You kept the initial 2 CRED by owing the faction that helped with the systems a favor, rather than paying them. No CRED was spent on extra status changes, and no crew-quality increase was purchased.

**Favorite contact:** Citani, a reclusive information broker. The particular contact relationships above are original campaign choices, not canonical claims about Citani. No faction is at war with you. The Cobalt favor is an obligation, not numeric debt; no invented ward-boss tithe applies.

## Crew XP

Review the four core triggers at session end: a successful transport/smuggling job; contending with challenges above your station; strengthening or changing your reputation; expressing the crew's goals, drives, internal conflict, or essential nature. Mark 1 XP for each that occurred, or 2 if it occurred repeatedly. Core pp. 51 and 122-125.

Crew quality, ship system quality, a faction's Tier, and a reputation label are different things. This campaign has no Blades-style crew Tier/hold/turf ladder or spendable Rep resource.
