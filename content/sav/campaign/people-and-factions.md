# People around the Quiet Orbit

These are the people and relationships the crew knows before the first job. Job-specific people and placements are original additions to the published Procyon sector.

## People

**Citani — reclusive information broker.** Your favorite Stardancer contact. Introduced Sen and vouches that the fee exists. Values discretion and reliable introductions, not obedience.

**Dr. Sen Ardan — civilian researcher.** Needs a living research specimen moved to medical care. Has pickup authority, a habitat manual, and an export dispute. Offers 6 CRED for live delivery or 3 for safe return if delivery proves impossible.

**Mira Dax — bonded-cargo clerk.** Can release the consignment. Wants a signed record she can defend to her supervisor. Treating her as a person is useful.

**Inspector Hale — local customs officer.** Can inspect outbound manifests and hold departure clearance. Has a team and a job to do. An authorization to collect cargo does not automatically clear export.

**Asha Venn — tug captain.** Runs a working tug between nearby platforms. Knows service lanes and values reciprocal favors. An ally is someone you negotiate with, not a free crew upgrade.

**Dr. Olan Vey — Lantern Clinic physician.** Works aboard an independent medical ship outside the inspection perimeter. Can provide legitimate care and receive the consignment. The clinic's neutrality is a practice it negotiates, not immunity from every faction.

## Starting factions

See the [ship record](/sav/wiki/quiet-orbit) for exact status and creation accounting. Published faction identities are summarized here; these particular relationships are campaign choices.

- **Cobalt Syndicate (+1):** labor organization and criminal enterprise. Helped improve the ship's systems; the crew owes a favor.
- **Mendicants (+1):** itinerant healers. Helped source the Medical Bay; the clinic is independent unless play establishes a connection.
- **Counters Guild (−2):** controls finance. Lost a disputed medical equipment deal when the ship acquired upgrades.
- **Conclave 01 (+1):** independent Urbots seeking liberation. Friendly through Citani in this campaign.
- **Church of Stellar Flame (−1):** an established religious power opposed to the crew's contact here.

Local customs is not a new sector-wide faction, and no starting faction is at war with the crew. Select a known site in the [route atlas](/sav/atlas/) for public access and jurisdiction notes. Faction presence does not imply exclusive ownership.
