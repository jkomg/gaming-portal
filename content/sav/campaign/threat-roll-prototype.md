# Experimental S&V Threat Rolls

**Design draft, not active rules. Not official. No table trial yet.** Read the compatibility assessment first. This is a deliberately constrained conversion of Deep Cuts pp. 91–107 for the supplied pregens; it is not a promise that every S&V ability is covered. Use it for a separate replayable encounter after learning core S&V. Agree all rulings before rolling.

## Resolution procedure

1. State the PC's action and intended effect. Normally the action achieves that effect while the roll determines its costs. If success itself is genuinely doubtful, announce a separate **failure threat**. Do not turn every routine task into one.
2. Announce each concrete threat and its full/reduced consequence before rolling: e.g. a 2-tick warning / 1-tick warning. Default risky/standard; no impending danger means no roll. A desperate roll awards 1 attribute XP and treats 1–5 as the full threat. The player decides whether to proceed and has final say on the action rating after discussing the fiction.
3. Roll the action rating plus bonus dice plus **one die for each threat after the first**. Assign a different result to each threat, then ignore leftover dice. One die cannot solve two threats. On 6 avoid that threat; on 4/5 suffer its reduced consequence or push to avoid it; on 1–3 suffer the full consequence or push to reduce it. Desperate removes the 4/5 reduced band. A failure threat gives reduced effect on 4/5 or failure on 1–3, with pushing improving it as the module describes.
4. **Push instead of resistance:** roll the attribute appropriate to the consequence. Multiple 6s cost 0 stress, highest 6 costs 1, 4/5 costs 2, 1–3 costs 3. At zero dice roll two keep lower, no critical. It automatically produces the stated improvement; it is a cost roll, not a second success test. Push against each consequence separately. To activate a push ability or improve effect proactively, allow the module's fixed 2-stress option instead. Do not also roll core resistance against the same consequence.
5. Core armor can handle an applicable incoming consequence. Relevant special armor that pays for pushing costs no stress and needs no push roll, with the ability's normal limits. Translate resistance-only bonus dice to the corresponding push-cost roll; bonuses to action rolls become Threat Roll bonuses. Quality and potency affect fictional effect and severity, not automatically dice.

**Zero rating, no bonuses:** first generate one result using 2d keep lower; add one ordinary result for each additional threat, then assign. **Zero rating with bonus dice:** for this proposal, total positive bonus dice become the base ordinary pool (one bonus means one ordinary die), then add the additional-threat dice. Do not add bonus dice to the special keep-lower pair as well. This explicit bonus handling is our implementation choice for the module's edge case.

## Gambits replace Edge in this proposal

There is **no Edge currency or reward**, including on multiple sixes. Keep the Stardancer pool resetting to 2 each job. Spend at most one Gambit for +1d on a PC's Threat Roll. Mystic The Way can instead substitute a Gambit for a stress cost, including an assist or push-cost result; record each such use. One activation replaces the stated whole cost, not one Gambit per stress. Ordinary spending still requires the crew's consent.

After every consequence and push from this resolution is settled, add at most **one** Gambit if the original Threat Roll was risky (not controlled or desperate), contained a 6 among its usable results, and **no Gambit was spent anywhere in that resolution**, including costs paid by The Way. A discarded high die still counts as a rolled usable result; the discarded higher die in a zero-rating keep-lower pair does not. Push-cost dice never generate Gambits. This stricter resolution-level exclusion is our anti-loop rule, not core S&V's exact wording.

This keeps the shared luck pool and Mystic's identity while avoiding double rewards. It does **not** prove a balanced regeneration rate: adding threats also adds dice and can make a 6 more likely. Do not invent harmless extra threats to farm luck. Log spent/generated Gambits, and compare with the core encounter. A pool that repeatedly refills without hard choices is a reason to revise the design.

Scoundrel **Serendipitous** adds one starting Gambit; it does not change generation thresholds. **Never Tell Me the Odds** is the separate ability allowing generation on desperate rolls and when a Gambit was spent. That explicit exception overrides our ordinary spend exclusion for an acting PC who has it, still capped at one earned Gambit per resolution. Record whether it makes the pool too plentiful before expanding the prototype. These pregens have neither ability.

## Bargains and teamwork

A bargain can exchange an agreed cost for a result without a roll, or add a real threat and its extra die to a roll. A threat is not also a guaranteed cost: if a die avoids it, it does not happen. Never charge both the old guaranteed bargain and the newly avoidable threat for one bonus. For this first prototype, **do not buy an extra Threat Roll die by a generic 2-stress push**; use a threat, a Gambit, an assist, or a specific ability. Pushing still increases effect and activates relevant abilities.

Assist: 1 stress for +1d to an ally's Threat Roll, with an explanation of exposure. Setups change the fictional danger or effect. Protect: take the ally's consequence as stated, then push against it if appropriate. For a coordinated group, each participant rolls for the threats they actually face; do not use core's best roll for everyone or charge leader stress per failed participant. A leader may push on someone's behalf when able to intervene, paying their own cost. This follows the module's approach and is a major spotlight change; keep group size small for the trial. A consequence targeting two characters must be announced as such, not copied secretly after assignment.

Gather information through the fiction; give accessible facts. If investigation itself is dangerous, name that threat. A long uncertain investigation can be a project; don't use a Threat Roll as a disguised mandatory clue gate. Engagement, flashbacks, ship procedures, and post-job core economy remain unless explicitly covered here.

## Worked encounter

Replay the service-latch scene without changing a live campaign outcome. Fuse has Hack 2. Effect: the latch opens. Announce two threats: **inspection alarm, 2 ticks reduced to 1**, and **arc burn, harm 2 reduced to harm 1**. Pool: 2 base +1 for the second threat = 3d. No failure threat; failure to open the latch is not a hidden third outcome.

Results **6, 4, 2**: Fuse assigns 6 to the alarm and 4 to the burn. The door opens, no alarm, harm 1 unless pushed. Fuse pushes using Prowess 1d, rolls 4, pays 2 stress, avoids the burn. No Gambit was spent, so the original risky 6 generates one **after** this resolves.

Alternative: Echo pays for that push using The Way when Echo is the acting character. The Gambit substitution means no Gambit is generated from that resolution, even though the original roll had a 6. If the roll was desperate, the 4 would be full harm 2 before pushing, and no ordinary Gambit would be generated.

At Hack 0 and no bonuses, two threats generate a keep-lower result plus one ordinary result. A paired **6 and 2** produces **2**, plus ordinary **5** gives assignable results 2 and 5. There is no rolled usable 6 and no critical. Spending a Gambit instead gives base 1d plus 1d for the second threat, with no regeneration allowed.

## Selected ability audit

| Character / ability | Prototype treatment |
|---|---|
| Drift: Ace Pilot | Speed potency unchanged; +1d to appropriate push-cost rolls for piloting consequences |
| Drift: Traveler | Potency when communicating with unusual cultures/xenos; not a bonus die |
| Fuse: Tinker | Add one progress segment on clocks worked with Rig/Hack or when studying a schematic, including during jobs; not a Threat Roll bonus die |
| Fuse: Bailing Wire and Mech-Tape | Core repair activity/cost exception unchanged; no effect on upkeep |
| Silk: Air of Respectability | Restricted extra downtime activity unchanged; lay low still needs another system |
| Silk: Player | Lie knowledge remains reliable; do not hide facts behind a Threat Roll |
| Anchor: Unstoppable | Push to perform superhuman force or match a small gang in close combat; activation changes fictional effect/scale and retains a normal push benefit, not harm reduction |
| Anchor: Bodyguard | Clear 1 stress when taking harm; +1d only to push-cost rolls when protecting a crewmate, following its original resistance trigger; not generic Threat Roll dice |
| Echo: The Way | One Gambit substitutes for a particular stress cost; resolution-level regeneration exclusion applies |
| Echo: Warded | Applicable special armor handles the relevant consequence without stress/push roll; resets when selecting new-job load as in S&V |
| Mercy: I'm a Doctor, Not a… | Substitution uses Doctor for the named action after paying activation push; not a free substitution every roll |
| Mercy: Physicker | Treatment and recovery unchanged; no global harm immunity |
| Crew: The Getaway | Potency for Scramble/Helm escape and +1d delivery engagement remain; no universal escape die |

**Other abilities, before selecting them:** Pilot **Punch It!** makes a desperate roll risky when a Gambit is spent; in this prototype restore the 4/5 reduced band and keep the no-generation condition unless Never Tell Me the Odds explicitly overrides it. **Commander** gives group scale and pooled criticals in combat: retain scale in the coordinated fiction, but its cross-player critical benefit needs a new agreed benefit because there is no shared best result here. Until written, use core for a Commander crew. S&V Scoundrel **Daredevil** offers +1d on a desperate roll instead of its attribute XP; retain that choice, not the Blades push penalty. Stitch **Under Pressure** adds a Gambit when a crew member actually suffers level 2+ harm after reductions; retain this separate harm trigger, not a resistance bonus, and log it separately from roll generation. Harm/recovery abilities need another pass if also enabling the Harm module. Any newly selected ability outside this audit must be reviewed before the next job.

## Minimum tests before campaign use

Replay: one risky threat; two threats; desperate; zero action with/without Gambit; one 6 versus multiple 6s; Mystic payment after a 6; two separate consequences and two pushes; protect with Bodyguard; Warded; a failure threat; an entirely safe action; a group escape. Check that every threat has an announced severity, gets its own result, and has one consequence resolution. Log stress, Gambits, roll count, explanation time, and each player's ability use.

Keep core payoff/downtime. Stop if players cannot state what success means before rolling. Revert at a session boundary, preserve earned outcomes, and write any resource changes. This prototype is a testable option, not evidence that Action ports cleanly.
