# Your first S&V job

**Active rules: core Scum and Villainy.** Deep Cuts options on other pages are inactive proposals. Printed core page numbers are ten less than the page number in the supplied full-text PDF.

## Action and consequence

Describe your goal and approach; choose the action. The GM explains position and effect before the roll. Roll d6s equal to the action rating, keep the highest. At zero dice, roll two and keep the lower; that cannot produce a critical. A 6 succeeds, a 4/5 succeeds with a consequence, and a 1-3 goes badly according to position. Multiple 6s give a critical when rolling a positive pool.

Before rolling, push for 2 stress for +1d or improved effect, or take an agreed Devil's Bargain for +1d. The push bonus die and bargain cannot stack. One ally may assist for 1 stress and +1d. The crew may spend one Gambit for another +1d. Fictional positioning, gear, setups, and special abilities also matter.

After hearing a consequence, resistance automatically reduces or avoids it as the GM determines. Roll the relevant attribute; stress cost is 6 minus the highest result, while a critical clears 1 stress. Attributes count the actions with a dot in their column, not all the dots. A resistance-only bonus does not increase vice dice. Suitable armor can also absorb a consequence when the fiction permits; mark the relevant box and item load.

## Gambits are shared

Reset the crew pool to **2** at the start of a job for this Stardancer. Any PC may spend one for +1d on an action. A risky action with a 6 or critical normally generates one Gambit if you did not spend one on that roll. Controlled or desperate rolls do not normally generate one. One qualifying roll grants one Gambit, not one per 6. Specific abilities can change these conditions.

Echo's Mystic starting ability can spend a Gambit instead of a stress cost, including a push or resistance. That is a separate permission from buying a bonus die; account for what was actually purchased. Never assume the crew has infinite Gambits. Core p. 121 and the Mystic rules apply.

## Flashbacks and teamwork

A flashback establishes earlier preparation for a stated stress cost and a roll if uncertain. It cannot erase a fact already established. A flashback to a downtime activity costs 1 CRED as normal. Assist, setup, protect, and group actions are available. A group-action leader takes 1 stress per participant who rolls 1-3; the group uses the best result. Nobody must make a group roll for a clear, safe exit.

## Equipment and space

Choose light 3 or normal 5 load; the supplied sheet offers heavy 6, within the core book's heavy 6-8 range. For this starter, use 6 if choosing heavy. Reveal items as needed rather than packing everything in advance. Refreshing load on the ship does not erase stress or harm; use the core gear procedure when there is time and access.

Fine gear improves relevant quality; it is not automatically +1d. A spacesuit permits EVA, but still needs its load and air supply. Ship storage is not free personal equipment while away from the ship. A case does not become a crew upgrade because you carried it once.

## After the job

Receive payoff, pay or skip upkeep, assess **Rin's** Heat, apply known faction changes, roll an entanglement from the wanted-level column for the system where the ship ends the job, then take downtime. At Wanted 0 this is a single d6 on the Wanted 0 table, not the Blades wanted-level dice pool. Heat reaches a new Wanted level at **8**, with overflow retained. Do not copy the Blades nine-Heat threshold.

Each PC normally has two downtime activities, one at war. Extra activities cost 1 CRED; there is no Rep payment option. Silk also has a restricted extra activity for acquire asset or lay low. Fuse has an extra free repair activity from their selected ability. Activities need fictional access. Laying low requires being in another system; a quick orbit around Warren is not enough.

Recovery clears level 1 harm after treatment; if harm remains, advance the **six-segment** healing clock. Filling it clears **all** harm; taking more harm before completion resets that clock. The Medical Bay adds +1d to recovery rolls. Ordinary repair costs 1 CRED and an activity per ship damage; Fuse's ability changes their repair cost and allowance. Core pp. 176-193.

Stress, harm, ship damage, debt, and per-system Heat persist. Review personal and crew XP triggers; do not reward only successful rolls. Desperate actions mark attribute XP when rolled. No pregen needs to invent trauma to earn XP.
