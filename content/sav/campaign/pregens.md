# Choose your crew

Six complete scoundrels; choose three to five. Take a role that sounds enjoyable rather than trying to cover every rule. Unchosen characters are away on personal business and contribute no extra turns, downtime activities, or abilities.

| Pregen | Role | Pick for |
|---|---|---|
| [Drift](/sav/wiki/pregen-drift) | Pilot | Daring approaches and escapes |
| [Fuse](/sav/wiki/pregen-fuse) | Mechanic | Hacking, engineering, and ship repairs |
| [Silk](/sav/wiki/pregen-silk) | Speaker | Negotiations, access, and catching lies |
| [Anchor](/sav/wiki/pregen-anchor) | Muscle | Protecting people and applying force |
| [Echo](/sav/wiki/pregen-echo) | Mystic | Way phenomena and strange encounters |
| [Mercy](/sav/wiki/pregen-mercy) | Stitch | Science, care, and recovery |

For three players, Drift/Fuse/Silk is a useful mix. No playbook is mandatory: another character can Helm, and the job has practical responses to the cargo's behavior without a Mystic or Stitch.

Every pregen has seven action dots, preserving the playbook's initial three and adding four with a maximum of 2 per action. Each also has the S&V **unique starting ability plus one chosen special ability**, resistance/vice ratings, gear, connections, and empty trackers. Keep the supplied Player Sheets open for marking boxes, or record changes on the pregen page. The Blank Muscle sheet is optional for someone building a custom character later; it is not a different playbook.

## Ten-minute pickup

Choose characters (3 minutes), read the [ship record](/sav/wiki/quiet-orbit) (2), agree boundaries and a pause signal (3), then introduce one crew tie each (2). Go straight to [the briefing](/sav/wiki/a-breathing-cargo). Save mechanical rebuilding and a full session zero for later.
