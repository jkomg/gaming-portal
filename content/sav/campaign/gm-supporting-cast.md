# GM supporting cast

Private cards. SPCs act through the fiction and the PCs' action/fortune rolls; they do not need PC stat blocks or their own combat turns. Quality is a starting comparison, not an automatic effect penalty. Ordinary dock security is quality I. Introduce stronger equipment openly before it matters.

## Citani — the introduction

Published Stardancer contact; this portrayal is original. **Wants:** introductions that leave everyone alive and still answering calls. **Knows:** Sen is solvent, the permit is disputed, the clinic is real; not Morrow's nature. **Leverage:** information, a discreet introduction to Asha. **Limit:** no power to erase customs records. **Reaction:** rewards candor with the next lead, grows wary of making civilians expendable. Voice: concise, careful questions. “What did they actually ask you to carry?”

## Dr. Sen Ardan — the client

**Wants:** the specimen alive under proper medical care. **Knows:** it reacts to electromagnetic and Way activity; no direct comms connection; has not identified sentience. **Leverage:** 6 CRED, pickup code, manual, fallback return arrangement. **Capability:** Study/medical expertise quality I; no fighting force. **Pressure:** professional reputation, competing claims on the consignment. **Reaction:** believes credible evidence of intelligence, agrees to patient treatment and the promised fee. Anger at a needless death, not an automatic betrayal. Player detects no hidden sale. “I can tell you the power tolerance. I can't tell you what it's dreaming.”

## Mira Dax — the desk

**Wants:** a clean handoff and an uneventful shift. **Knows:** clearance queue, service access, who can authorize exceptions. **Leverage:** release timestamp and a call to Hale. **Limit:** cannot authorize export herself. **Reaction:** a credible guarantor or signed medical urgency gets consideration; intimidation may gain compliance with a recorded alarm. Won't fight to the death over a desk. “Whose name goes in this field?”

## Inspector Hale — the border

**Wants:** a documented resolution without embarrassing the office. **Knows:** an irregular research consignment is leaving; not the occupant's intelligence. **Leverage:** inspection hold, two officers, scanner, lane control. **Capability:** quality I, sidearms and armor only if revealed; no cruiser or supernatural detection. **Reaction:** an honestly negotiated escort can succeed; false records invite scrutiny when inconsistencies appear. Pursues an actual dangerous departure, but accepts a clear concession. “Show me where this transfer ends.”

## Asha Venn — another route

**Wants:** working pilots to help one another; fuel and a reciprocal tow favor. **Knows:** local tug routes and the clinic's position. **Leverage:** legitimate tug schedule, mechanical couplers. **Capability:** a small civilian tug, quality I; not an armed ship. **Reaction:** accepts a reasonable favor, declines being a human shield without warning, may set up the crew's route. Her cooperation does not require adding a permanent cohort. “You need a tow, or you need someone to look like they're towing you?”

## Dr. Olan Vey — the destination

**Wants:** patients stabilized and the clinic still able to operate tomorrow. **Knows:** containment medicine, not a complete theory of the Way. **Leverage:** medical receiving record and safe habitat. **Capability:** treatment quality I; the crew's own Med Bay is separate. **Reaction:** offers a practical alternative if the case opens; refuses invasive experiments without consent. Doesn't demand the crew join a faction. “A history first. Who has been deciding what it needs?”

## Morrow — not inventory

**Wants:** quiet, safe power, and choices about where it goes. **Knows:** recurring signals, a remembered habitat, a broken fragment of navigation data. **Capability:** bioelectric signaling through adjacent connected maintenance systems; no arbitrary remote possession. **Pressure:** repeated interrogation pulses. **Reaction:** copies patterns when scared; cooperates with repeated gentle signals. Full distress clock opens the service housing, not lethal harm. It can communicate a preference through displays, gestures, or Attune; the crew need not roll a Mystic to grant it care. If using an animal, replace intent and navigation testimony with trained recognition of a signal.

## Rook Hessa — optional next-session courier

**Wants:** a paying route contract before the Guild captures it. **Knows:** someone is bidding on an incomplete navigation archive. **Leverage:** another ship and one access code. **Reaction:** a rival who can become a partner; no compulsory betrayal. Keep Rook out of the teaching job unless there is spare time. Introduce at debrief as one possible next lead, not a surprise fourth obstacle.
