# A Breathing Cargo

**Player briefing | 2-3 hour teaching session | Rin system**

Citani, your trusted information broker, introduces **Dr. Sen Ardan**, a civilian researcher. Sen needs a sealed habitat case collected from **Kestrel Transfer**, a small bonded freight platform near Warren, and delivered to **Lantern Clinic**, an independent medical ship already waiting outside the platform's inspection perimeter.

Sen is explicit: the case contains a **living specimen**. It needs stable power, gentle handling, and no direct connection to the ship's comms. The contents belong to a disputed research consignment, so a customs team is reviewing its export permit. You have a pickup authorization and the habitat manual, not an exemption from inspection.

**Pay:** 6 CRED for delivering the intact case and its live occupant to the clinic. If you cannot make the handoff, Sen offers 3 CRED for returning the living specimen safely to their care. This fallback is agreed before departure. Your crew pays 1 CRED ship upkeep afterward, from its funds; that is not a hidden reduction in Sen's price.

**The route:** collect from the transfer platform, cross a local customs inspection corridor, and reach the clinic. All three are in Rin; no jumpgate or inter-system trip is required. You already have a ship that can make the journey.

**The case:** about the size of a large instrument trunk. Two handles; 2 personal load if carried by one person, or 1 each if carried together. In the cargo hold it takes cargo space, not anyone's personal load. Carrying it is not a separate roll unless the situation makes it risky. It is already powered for the planned journey.

**Known approaches:** use the public freight desk with the authorization, negotiate with the bonded-cargo clerk, or enter through a maintenance access with suitable preparation. A transport plan needs a route and means; other plans are welcome. Choose load, answer a couple of useful questions, roll engagement, and begin at the first obstacle.

The station staff are doing a job, not waiting to die for a container. How quietly do you want to handle this?
